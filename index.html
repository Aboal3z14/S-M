Good to Know

Q&A

What should I wear?

Cocktail attire. The ceremony and reception are outdoors, so flat shoes are a good idea.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely — we'd love for your little ones to join us and celebrate the day together.

Can I bring a plus one?

Please check your invitation for your plus-one status, or reach out to us directly.

What's the weather like?

October days are mild and evenings cool down, so consider bringing a light layer.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The celebration takes place indoors, though there's a pleasant outdoor space just outside the hall, perfect if you'd like to pop out for a breath of fresh air.

Can we use our phones and cameras to take photos during the wedding?

Absolutely! You're most welcome to use phones and cameras. We'd be delighted to see the day captured from your perspective. (don't forget to share them with us too)
